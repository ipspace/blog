[Garrett Masters](https://www.linkedin.com/in/garrett-masters-ba6234101/) published a great article describing how [he uses Claude Code in his networking labs](https://gtalkstech.com/blog/claude-code-network-engineers/). I'm pretty sure you can find tons of similar articles[^SASL], but I liked this one[^PPNL] because it's not a hyperventilated pile of hype.

The blog post includes the guardrails Garrett uses, the details of what he does in a lab that he wouldn't do in a production network, adaptations you might have to do to make his skills usable in your network, and concludes with *Honest Limitations* (what I'm missing most in AI gospels), including the "*when your network is down, your AI is probably down as well*" caveat.
