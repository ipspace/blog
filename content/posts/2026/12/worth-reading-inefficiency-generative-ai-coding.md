[Gerben Wierda](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gerbenwierda/) decided to test how well coding with AI works. He let AI create a pretty complex application (while controlling its architecture and design decisions), and documented the process in a long sequence of blog posts (links at the end of [this one](https://ea.rna.nl/2026/11/17/serious-coding-with-llms-lab-notes-2026-11-17-wait-11000-lines-generated-for-every-line-of-code-of-my-system/)).

As he concluded the project, he looked at how many tokens were consumed and how many lines of final code were generated. The ratio is almost too crazy to believe:

> For every single line of code in my product, the LLM had to generate the equivalent of roughly 11,000 of them.

I guess that never bothered the "_let's add another layer of indirection so we can fire a few more engineers_" crowd; it's only the old farts like myself that care.

If you want to know more, read the [unimaginable inefficiency of coding with Generative AI](https://ea.rna.nl/2026/11/17/serious-coding-with-llms-lab-notes-2026-11-17-wait-11000-lines-generated-for-every-line-of-code-of-my-system/) blog post.
