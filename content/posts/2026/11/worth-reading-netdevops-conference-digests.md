Roman Dodin launched a [phenomenal website](https://tldr.netdevops.me/) summarizing networking-, cloud-, and AI- related conferences ([list of tracked conferences](https://tldr.netdevops.me/how-it-works/#which-conferences-are-tracked)).   Every Monday, his ~~minions~~ AI agents scan the conference webpages and create event digests ([behind-the-scenes details](https://tldr.netdevops.me/how-it-works/#the-pipeline)), resulting in per-event pages like the summary of the [CHNUG#5 event](https://tldr.netdevops.me/chnug-5/) I attended last week.

Thanks a million, Roman, for creating this masterpiece, which also happens to be one of the best uses of AI I've encountered so far.
