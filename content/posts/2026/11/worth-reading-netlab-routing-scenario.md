In his [Labs as Code, Part 2: Real Images, Real Topology](https://routerjockey.com/labs-as-code-part-2/) article, [Tony Mattke](https://routerjockey.com/about/) described how he used [netlab](https://netlab.tools/) to recreate a customer routing scenario.

His article starts with the basics (importing an Arista cEOS container), and then slowly walks you through the process of building a netlab file that results in an 8-router network running BGP, BFD, OSPF, and a few routing policies. Absolutely marvelous reading if you're new to netlab or exploring its capabilities.
