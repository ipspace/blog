With the [growing number](/2026/10/netlab-26-10/) of SRv6 implementations that [_netlab_ supports](https://netlab.tools/module/srv6/#module-srv6-platform), it made sense to create a [set of topologies](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples) to help you get started exploring the SRv6 world. The sample topologies include:

- [SRv6 with IS-IS](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/1-intro/1-isis) including [LLA-only core links](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/1-intro/2-unnumbered)
- [Multivendor SRv6](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/1-intro/3-multivendor)
- [IPv4 Islands over SRv6-Enabled IPv6 Core](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/2-services/1-ipv4-islands)
- [IPv6 BGP-Free Core with SRv6](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/2-services/2-bgp-free-ipv6)
- [IPv4 L3VPN over SRv6-Enabled IPv6 Core](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/2-services/3-mpls-vpn)

Read the [getting started](https://github.com/ipspace/srv6-examples/blob/main/docs/use.md) document and have fun exploring the scenarios 😎
