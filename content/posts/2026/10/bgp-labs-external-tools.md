After Kazuya Takahashi released his wonderful [network-xray](https://github.com/rclab-dev/network-xray) tool and [added it](https://github.com/bgplab/bgplab/pull/82) as an external tool to the [open-source BGP configuration labs](https://bgplabs.net/), I wrote a short document [explaining how to start external tools in your BGP labs](https://bgplabs.net/5-tools/) without changing the lab topology, and how it works in GitHub Codespaces (hint: wonderfully).

I also modified all lab instructions to include a pointer to the optional tool-setup step.

Happy labbing ;)
