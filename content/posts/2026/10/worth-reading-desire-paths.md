Chris Siebenmann [publishes](https://utcc.utoronto.ca/~cks/space/blog/) articles written from an interesting perspective: he's a system administrator at a university (based on my ancient, similar experience, that's usually worse than herding cats).

In one of his recent articles, he applied the [desire paths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desire_path) ideas to IT: [don't fight how people are doing things](https://utcc.utoronto.ca/~cks/space/blog/sysadmin/UniversityPeopleDoStuff); either kindly nudge them in the right direction, or help them get stuff done the way they like to get it done, but in the least harmful way. I could definitely use some of his wisdom decades ago 🤷🏻‍♂️
