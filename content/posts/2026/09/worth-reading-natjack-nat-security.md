Every time I wrote something along the lines of *[NAT is not a security feature](/2011/12/is-nat-security-feature/)*, I got the expected pushback from people defending their ~~bad~~ suboptimal design choices.

Fortunately, rational[^BA] engineers no longer need to have that discussion: the [NatJack](https://natjack.io/) website documents[^SH] a half-dozen attacks on typical NAT implementations.

[^BA]: Yeah, I know that's a pretty high bar 😜

[^SH]: With the expected dose of _the world is burning_ security hype

Obvious next step: coping mechanisms like "_this is all theoretical_", like the "_[but the remote host cannot reply](https://blog.ipspace.net/2012/11/ipv6-router-advertisements-deep-dive/#3062)_" argument made 23 years after the [Slammer worm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SQL_Slammer) 🤦‍♂️.
