We had a wonderful SR-MPLS workshop @ [ITNOG 10](https://www.itnog.it/itnog10/) in Bologna on April 20th 2025. If you missed it, explore:

* The [Segment Routing: from Theory to Practice](https://www.itnog.it/itnog10/files/TRaining_Segment%20Routing%20ITNOG%202026%20april%2020_signed.pdf) presentation by [Tiziano Tofoni](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tiziano-tofoni-1361759/)
* The [slide deck](https://my.ipspace.net/bin/get/SR101/Segment%20Routing%20Workshop.pdf?doccode=SR101) for the hands-on part of the workshop
* The [lab topologies](https://github.com/ipspace/SR-workshop), and the [usage guidelines](https://github.com/ipspace/SR-workshop/blob/main/docs/use.md).
