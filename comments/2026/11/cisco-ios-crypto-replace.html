    I think that this goes beyond a timing issue and Cisco is well aware of it, see https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/routers/ios/config/17-x/syst-mgmt/b-system-management/m_cm-config-rollback-0.html

    > When using the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) for signature certificate validation, the copy startup-config running-config and the configure replace commands, are not supported. A device reload is needed when you replace or load configuration instructions from a different file.

    Fun times when a few years ago I was tasked with fully automating PKI enrollment of Cisco routers for a customer. At first this "configuration replace" feature seemed like a nice idea, until I actually tried it, miserably failed, finally found the aforementioned Cisco doc, and ended up with good old "copy cfgfile startup-config" + reload.

    Configuration management on IOS(-XE) seems absolutely broken.
