    I often see it in dual internet provider designs where they don't want to run an extra IGP between IBGP peers because most of the time there are only two routers. They also don't want to lower BGP timers (that also needs more CPU cycles). They use BFD to detect intermediate path failures because most of the time there's is no direct connection between IBGP peers. BFD is more lightweight than any IGP. BFD timers like 3 x 300 msec are not much of a burden for today's router CPUs. So there is a design where it makes sense. Don't think you know it all.
